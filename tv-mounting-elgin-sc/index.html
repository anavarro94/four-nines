Tv mounting FAQ

Do I need to buy the mount?

You can buy your own mount, or we can pick one up for you. If we buy it, the cost of the mount is due before we buy it.

Can you hide the wires?

Yes. We can run them inside the wall or cover them with a paintable cord cover, depending on the wall.

Can you mount over a fireplace?

Usually, yes. Send a photo of the fireplace wall and we will tell you what it takes.

How can I pay?

Cash, Zelle, Venmo or Apple Pay.
