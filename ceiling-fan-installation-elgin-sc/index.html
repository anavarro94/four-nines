Ceiling fan installation FAQ

Can you replace a light with a fan?

Yes. We check that the ceiling box can hold a fan and swap it for a fan-rated box if it cannot.

Do I need to buy the fan?

Yes, or tell us which one you want and we can pick it up. If we buy it, the fan cost is due before we buy it.

My fan wobbles. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Most wobbles come from loose mounting or unbalanced blades.
